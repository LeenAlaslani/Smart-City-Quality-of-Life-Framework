## Project Overview

Cities manage large amounts of data related to transportation, energy, public services, and waste collection. However, this data is often separated between departments and mainly used after problems have already happened.

**CityPulse AI** transforms city data into forecasts, priorities, and clear recommendations that can support earlier and better decisions.

The project combines four independent machine-learning modules inside one Smart City framework. Each module studies a different part of city quality of life using real urban data.

---

## The Problem

City departments may face several challenges:

- Transportation risks are difficult to anticipate.
- Energy demand can increase during unexpected periods.
- Public-service requests may remain unresolved for a long time.
- Waste demand changes between months and districts.
- Decision makers may receive technical data without clear recommended actions.

These challenges can affect city resources, service quality, sustainability, and residents’ quality of life.

---

## Our Solution

CityPulse AI provides one decision-support workspace for four Smart City areas:

- Smart Transportation
- Smart Energy
- Smart Public Services
- Smart Waste Management

The platform uses machine-learning models to produce forecasts and then converts the results into:

- Clear priority levels
- Simple explanations
- Recommended city actions
- Executive summaries
- AI-supported action plans

The purpose is not only to show predictions, but to help decision makers understand:

> **What may happen, why it matters, and what the city should do next.**

## Smart City Modules

| Notebook | Smart City Area | Main Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **01. Smart London Urban Risk Prediction** | Transportation and Mobility | Identify urban conditions and time periods with higher transportation risk |
| **02. Smart Building Energy Prediction** | Energy and Sustainable Buildings | Forecast building electricity consumption and identify high-demand periods |
| **03. Smart Governance and Digital Services** | Public Services | Identify service requests that may require more attention because of long resolution time |
| **04. Smart Waste Collection Demand Prediction** | Environment and Waste Management | Forecast monthly waste demand for each district to support collection planning |

Each notebook contains its own dataset, machine-learning models, evaluation, findings, and Smart City recommendations.

The raw datasets are not merged because every city area represents a different operational problem. Their results are connected through the CityPulse AI platform.
